Beam-Fix

Notched glulam header → steel side-plate reinforcement. Change any input; results update live.

Exploration tool, not an engineered design. This is a simplified beam finite-element model with NDS-style fastener capacities. A tension-face notch in a structural glulam is a serious defect; any real repair of a load-bearing member must be reviewed and stamped by a licensed structural engineer.
